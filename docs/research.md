# Selection and research: Scoro

Checked 8 October 2026. New registry family: agency-operations. Industry: professional-firms. The build focuses on the agency's delivery and commercial review, distinct from generic time billing, MSP ticket/service operations and architecture practice administration already in the registry.

The annual-bill model is AUD 51,300 for 50 users at the vendor's displayed AUD 85.50 per user per month. It is an arithmetic scenario, not an observed customer invoice, and excludes add-ons, tax and onboarding. Public page figures use the published per-user amount only.

Scoro won on a documented work-report export and a bounded build of agency rituals. Cognidox's starting annual price is GBP 6,000, and migration requires deeper document handling. Intellect lists a starting figure of $26k, but the inspected page did not establish an annual term for that figure. Its regulated-industry scope is also a poor fit for this session. These are research scores, not a quantified market-demand model. Cognidox's bill score reflects a small five-figure NZD/AUD equivalent without claiming a researched exchange rate. Intellect's bill score is provisional and it was not selected.

No Scoro, Cognidox or Intellect usage appeared in the searched local customer and CRM materials. No live CRM evidence, named buyer invoice or measured keyword volume was verified. Buyer score stays at 2. Scoro's partner directory includes Australian delivery partners; that is market presence, not evidence of EDNA customer usage. Search results contain competing Scoro alternative pages, including vendor comparisons; search score is 3, not a volume claim.
